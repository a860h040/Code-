Question: Apixaban 5 mg PO twice daily is ordered for VTE prophylaxis after left total hip arthroplasty. Is this order appropriate? If not, what should be changed? Correct answer: No. Change to apixaban 2.5 mg PO twice daily. For hip replacement prophylaxis, treatment is typically continued for 35 days and started after adequate hemostasis, generally 12–24 hours after surgery. Question: Ketorolac 30 mg IV every 6 hours scheduled is ordered for postoperative pain in this 83-year-old patient. Is this order appropriate? Correct answer: No. For a patient ≥65 years old, reduce to ketorolac 15 mg IV every 6 hours, with a maximum of 60 mg/day. Use for the shortest necessary duration and no longer than 5 days. Question: Potassium chloride 20 mEq IV push once is ordered for a potassium level of 3.2 mmol/L. Is this order appropriate? Correct answer: No. Potassium chloride should never be given by IV push. Change it to a diluted IV infusion, such as KCl 20 mEq IVPB over approximately 2 hours, according to hospital policy and IV access. Question: Ondansetron 16 mg IV every 6 hours PRN is ordered for postoperative nausea and vomiting. Is this order appropriate? Correct answer: No. The dose is too high for routine postoperative nausea/vomiting. Change to ondansetron 4 mg IV PRN. Question: Acetaminophen 1,000 mg PO every 8 hours scheduled is ordered for postoperative multimodal pain control. Is this order appropriate? Correct answer: Yes. Verify as written. The total daily dose is 3,000 mg/day. Confirm that the patient is not receiving additional acetaminophen-containing medications. Question: Senna-docusate 2 tablets PO nightly is ordered for constipation prevention while receiving postoperative opioid therapy. Is this order appropriate? Correct answer: Yes. Verify as written. This is an appropriate postoperative bowel regimen.
